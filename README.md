# 🇮🇳 Digital India Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The **Digital India Dashboard** is a Data Science and Business Intelligence project that analyzes India's digital transformation from **2016 to 2025**.

This project combines key indicators such as GDP growth, internet adoption, smartphone penetration, literacy rate, and UPI transactions to understand how technology has transformed India's economy and society.

The project includes data collection, cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), forecasting, and an interactive Streamlit dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

**How has India changed over the last decade through digital transformation?**

This project explores the growth of:

* 🌐 Internet Users
* 💰 GDP
* 💳 UPI Transactions
* 📱 Smartphone Adoption
* 📚 Literacy Rate

and identifies trends, relationships, and future projections.

## 📊 Key Insights

### Internet Adoption

* Internet usage increased significantly between 2016 and 2025.
* Digital connectivity expanded rapidly across India.

### Smartphone Growth

* Smartphone adoption showed strong growth and closely aligned with internet expansion.

### UPI Revolution

* UPI transactions experienced exponential growth.
* India became one of the world's leading digital payment ecosystems.

### Economic Growth

* GDP increased alongside digital transformation.
* Technology adoption contributed to economic development.

### Digital Inclusion

* Literacy rate improvements supported broader access to digital services.
